All five screens, both sizes, both themes.

This is the real thing, not pictures of it. Tap a story to open it. Change the reading length. Switch tabs. Toggle a source. Every headline, outlet and link is a genuine AI story from 9–16 September 2026, and every “Read at” link opens the actual article.

Two labels, never one

What kind of thing it is sits on the left in plain small capitals. How well-sourced it is sits on the right in a bordered chip with a four-bar meter and the word. Different shape, different place, different job. Neither is ever carried by colour alone.

Orange means live

The reading length you are on, the flag where you stopped, the tab you are on, and the current hour on the Radar chart. Nothing else in the product is allowed to be orange.

The charts are measurements

Pickup shows how many outlets carried a story and how fast. The ranking bars show why it placed where it did, including the penalty pushing it down. Arrivals is the real ingest, hour by hour.

Bounded on purpose

Five minutes gives four stories, ten gives eight, Everything gives the lot. The count and the minutes are stated up front. There is no infinite scroll anywhere.
