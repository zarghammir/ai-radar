AI Radar · issue #11 · the Select Rail, calmed

Same world. Out of the way of the reading.

You picked the Select Rail and said it was too busy to read. You were right. The world is unchanged — film bench, orange and black, your place held with a flag. What changed is that the news is now the biggest thing on the screen, the machinery went quiet, and the pictures that are left are real measurements rather than decoration.

Two things you asked for are in now. Every story has a small pickup curve showing how many outlets carried it and how fast, the story page shows why it ranked as a bar breakdown, and Live Radar charts arrivals by hour. And yes — every card and every source in the list is a real link straight to the original article.

What I took out

  • was

    A rail between every story

    Now one continuous strip of sprocket holes runs down the left edge, the way film actually works. Nothing cuts across your reading any more.

  • was

    A row of orange dots

    Gone. They encoded importance and you could not read them. Importance is now simply that the lead story is bigger.

  • was

    Cryptic marks for trust

    Now a small four-bar meter plus the words. Four bars means the company said it itself. One bar means it is a rumour. Readable without learning anything.

  • was

    Orange in seven places

    Now orange appears only where something is live: the reading mode you are in, the flag where you stopped, and the tab you are on. Nowhere else.

  • was

    Small windows on a big black field

    Flipped. The paper card is now the main surface and the black is just the bench it sits on. You read on paper, not in a slot.

  • was

    Squeezed capitals everywhere

    Condensed type is now only used for tiny labels. Headlines and body text are set in a normal, roomy face at a larger size.
